Official Protocol & Step-by-Step Guide: Student Residence Permit (İkamet) Renewal & Health Insurance Compliance in North Cyprus

JM
Jonathan Mukendi verified

Secrétariat Général & Affaires Consulaires

October 12, 2025 · 6 min read

Pursuant to executive circular no. TRNC-MINT-2025/44 issued by the Ministry of Interior in Lefkoşa, all international students registered at Final International University must ensure their online student residency permits (İkamet) are formally completed prior to the statutory grace period expiring on November 15, 2025.

1. Mandatory Documents & FIU Registrar Attestation

Before initiating your digital portal submission at icisleri.gov.ct.tr, ensure you have obtained the certified physical and scanned electronic copies of the following documents:

1

Active Student Certificate (Öğrenci Belgesi)

Issued strictly within the last 15 days by the FIU Registrar's Office with active QR-code verification.

2

Certified Dormitory Lease or Mukhtar Residence Letter

Stamp and signature from your local district Mukhtar (Muhtar Belgesi) or official on-campus housing contract.

3

Valid Democratic Republic of Congo Passport

Must possess a minimum residual validity of at least six (6) months from the submission date.

“No Congolese scholar abroad should experience administrative vulnerability due to regulatory confusion. The community stands as a collective shield, ensuring that legal status and dignity are preserved for every brother and sister.”

— Executive Address, FIUCC General Assembly 2025

2. Digital Health Insurance Verification & Blood Work Protocol

Payment for the government medical examination fee (approximately 1,480 TL) must be rendered via credit or debit card on the ministry portal. First-year scholars must undergo mandatory biometric blood screenings and chest X-rays at Lefkoşa Burhan Nalbantoğlu State Hospital or authorized Girne clinics.

support_agent

FIUCC Legal Desk In-Person Support

The FIUCC Legal Desk is available every Wednesday from 14:00 to 17:00 in Student Union Room B2 for dossier review, document translation, and accompaniment to immigration appointments.

Once your health report is approved digitally, the final residency permit certificate will be generated electronically. Download the PDF and maintain both a laminated physical copy and a smartphone backup at all times.
