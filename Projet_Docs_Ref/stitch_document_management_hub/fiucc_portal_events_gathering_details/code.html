About This Gala

Each year on June 30th, Congolese students across Northern Cyprus gather to honor the proclamation of our national independence. Organised by the FIUCC Executive Bureau in collaboration with the Student Activities Directorate, the 2025 Gala stands as the highest diplomatic and cultural event on our student calendar.

The evening brings together university officials, foreign diplomats, faculty members, and student delegations from all major universities in the TRNC. Expect an unforgettable display of our rich heritage—from authentic Rumba and traditional folk dance orchestrations to high-level keynote addresses, research awards, and the formal recognition of our graduating Class of 2025 scholars.

Event Schedule & Program

Standard EEST Time
18:00 – 19:00

Red Carpet Reception & Welcome Cocktail

Guest arrival at the Grand Foyer, official registration credential badge check, cocktail drinks, and photographic portrait session.

19:00 – 19:45

National Anthems & Presidential Keynote

Solemn playing of "Debout Congolais", ceremonial welcome address by FIUCC President Jonathan Mukendi, followed by FIU Rectorate representative address.

19:45 – 21:00

Cultural Choreography & Live Congolese Rumba Set

Performance by the FIUCC Cultural Troupe highlighting the cultural tapestry of the 26 provinces, followed by live orchestra tribute to Franco, Tabu Ley, and Papa Wemba.

21:00 – 22:00

Academic Honors & Leadership Recognition

Conferral of community sash awards for Class of 2025 graduates, Dean’s High Honor commendations, and presentation of the Annual Leadership Medal.

22:00 – 23:30

Gastronomic Dinner Buffet & Diaspora Networking

Full course traditional Congolese banquet served on the Amphitheatre terrace, followed by informal community dance floor and DJ set.

Institutional Guidelines & Entry Protocol

Security Notice from the General Secretariat
  • badge Valid Student ID: Entry is strictly regulated. All registered students must present their physical FIU Student Card or official national passport at the registration desk.
  • styler Dress Code: Traditional Congolese gala attire (Liputa, Abacost, boubou) or formal evening black-tie suits and evening gowns. Casual jeans, trainers, and sportswear are strictly forbidden.
  • schedule Punctuality: Auditorium doors will close promptly at 18:45 for the formal national anthem ceremony and will not reopen until the keynote conclusion.
