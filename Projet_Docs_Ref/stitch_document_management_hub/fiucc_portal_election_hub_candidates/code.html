Election Rules & Democratic Charter (CENI-FIUCC)

Codified regulations enforcing fairness, neutrality, and inviolable anonymity for all diaspora voters.

01. Universal Suffrage & Single Voter Token Protocol expand_more

Every fully registered Congolese student at FIU receives exactly one unique, cryptographically signed voting token. Tokens cannot be regenerated once cast, and duplicate IP requests are monitored by the independent cybersecurity team.

02. Campaign Ethics & Digital Fair Play expand_more

Candidates are strictly prohibited from defamatory behavior, unsolicited late-night mass texting, or voter coercion. Official debates hosted by CENI are mandatory for eligibility. Violation results in immediate disqualification.

03. Ballot Secrecy & Anonymity Architecture expand_more

Student identities are decoupled from the ballot table through a zero-knowledge hash mechanism. CENI administrators, university staff, and technical volunteers have no visibility into how any individual student voted.

04. Quorum & Majority Threshold Requirements expand_more

The presidential ballot requires an absolute majority (50% + 1 vote) of participating eligible voters to validate the mandate. If no contender clears 50%, an expedited 24-hour run-off between the top two candidates is triggered.

05. Dispute Resolution & Electoral Litigation Window expand_more

Following the provisional ballot tally, any candidate or group of 10 voters may submit an official contestation to the CENI Legal Board within a 48-hour window before final institutional certification.
